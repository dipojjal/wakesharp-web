## Alarmy — alar.my/en/blog (74 English articles)

Publishing since ~2024, currently 2–4 posts/month, ~1,500–2,000 words each.

### Their editorial template (used almost verbatim in ~80% of posts)

1. **Hook:** a relatable failure scene (ten alarms dismissed, 2 AM doomscroll, waking with no memory of dismissing) — never a stat or definition.
2. **Reframe:** the problem is biological/neurological, not a character flaw ("you're not lazy").
3. **Mechanism:** sleep-science explainer (sleep inertia, habituation, prefrontal cortex, chronotypes) — vocabulary is used confidently but sources are rarely linked.
4. **Numbered fixes:** "5 causes", "6 tips", "4 habits" — quantified titles throughout, often year-stamped "(2026)".
5. **Product tie-in:** one *specific* feature matched to the article's pain point (melatonin timing → Habit Alarm; heat-wrecked sleep → Sleep Analysis), introduced only after 2–4 educational sections. Download banner + QR code appear ~2–3× per article.
6. **FAQ close:** ~58% of posts end with 3–5 search-phrased Q&As (featured-snippet / People-Also-Ask play). Brand essays, review spotlights and viral roundups skip it.

## What WakeSharp can own (gaps informing the 50-article plan)

1. **Morning cognition, measured** — nobody covers "how sharp are you after waking" (reaction time, warm-up games, Sharpness Score vs. your own baseline). Alarmy stops at "wake up"; Wayk floats into generic motivation. The space between — *wake up → be cognitively ready* — is empty and is literally the brand.
2. **Meeting-readiness / calendar-aware waking** — zero competitor coverage. Professional-morning content (first-meeting stress, interview days, working backward from the calendar).
3. **Honest reliability engineering** — Alarmy troubleshoots too, but WakeSharp's reliability checker ("it will ring, it may not, or it cannot") supports a distinctive *honesty* angle: what the platform can't promise, battery-optimizer killers, why "trust me" alarms are a lie.
4. **Privacy** — on-device sleep/calendar/camera data, no accounts. Neither competitor touches "your sleep tracker knows too much."
5. **Commitment-device psychology** — Strict Mode, snooze caps that cost Sharpness, streak freezes: real behavioral-econ content (Ulysses contracts, loss aversion) vs. Wayk's motivational fluff.
6. **Social accountability** — wake-up pacts and challenges; Wayk covers accountability abstractly, Alarmy not at all.
7. **Uncovered topics with real search volume:** revenge bedtime procrastination · morning anxiety · new-parent (adult) sleep · alarm anxiety · doomscrolling-to-bed pipeline · "why am I dumb for 30 minutes after waking."

**Tactics to adopt from Alarmy:** FAQ blocks on troubleshooting/science posts (snippet play) · numbered, query-matched titles · pain-point hooks + biological reframe · one specific feature tie-in per article, never a generic ad · internal cross-links between related posts.
**Tactics to reject:** unsourced science claims (WakeSharp cites real studies — trust is the brand) · keyword-cannibalizing near-duplicate posts · "trusted by millions" puffery (also banned by our copy linter).
