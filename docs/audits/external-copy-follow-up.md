## Suggested short copy

WakeSharp is a loud alarm clock with missions that give you something to do when it rings. Choose math, a photo, object scanning or walking, then plan your mornings with calendar alarms and shift rotations. Available for iPhone and Android. Feature availability differs by platform and version.

## Pricing copy

WakeSharp Unlimited includes the app's features without in-app ads. Eligible new subscribers can try the yearly plan free for 7 days, then pay US $34.99 per year. The monthly plan is US $4.99 without a trial. Local prices appear in the store. Subscriptions renew until cancelled.

## Platform qualification

On supported iPhones, WakeSharp uses AlarmKit and needs alarm permission. On Android, it uses the alarm audio stream and needs the relevant permissions and battery settings. Do Not Disturb must allow alarms. No alarm app can sound while the phone is powered off or out of battery.
