Retake the photo of the spot you chose the night before.

## How to use it

1. Choose Photo Proof and take a clear reference photo of your chosen spot.
2. Use a spot with stable lighting and recognisable details, such as a shelf.
3. Return and frame the same spot when the alarm rings. Scan an Object is a separate mission for recognising an object category.

- **Photo Proof**: Retake the photo of the spot you chose the night before.
- **Fetch**: Go and find something blue, or something you drink from.
- **Face Check**: Open your eyes for the camera, then follow the prompt.
- **Fruit Slash**: Slice the fruit out of the air with your finger.

## What to check

No WakeSharp account is required. Optional sign-in with Apple or Google backs up alarms, history, preferences and small photo-target thumbnails. Camera matching and calendar processing happen on your device. Calendar event content stays there. Read the privacy policy for the full details.
