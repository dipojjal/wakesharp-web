Choose a target such as a bottle, mug or sink. When the alarm rings, point the camera at that object. The green match confirmation tells you when WakeSharp has recognised it. Object recognition runs on your phone.

## How to use it

1. Choose Scan an Object in the mission picker and select a target.
2. Put the target somewhere you can reach safely after getting up.
3. Point the camera at it in good light. Hold it steady until the match turns green.

## What to check

No WakeSharp account is required. Optional sign-in with Apple or Google backs up alarms, history, preferences and small photo-target thumbnails. Camera matching and calendar processing happen on your device. Calendar event content stays there. Read the privacy policy for the full details.
