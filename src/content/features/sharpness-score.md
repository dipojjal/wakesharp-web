Sharpness is a daily in-app score. Review it alongside your history, streaks and badges, then visit Nest for your progress with Lark. It is not a sleep score or a medical assessment. A single number does not tell you whether you are safe to drive or work.

## How to use it

1. Finish your morning and open the Sharpness view.
2. Try an optional brain warm-up when you want to. Review the score breakdown shown in the app.
3. Look at several mornings in your history. Use badges and Nest as encouragement, not as a health diagnosis.

## What to check

The brain warm-up is optional. Math Problems, Memory Match and Sequence Recall can also be alarm missions. Word Dash and Reaction Tap are warm-up games, not choices in the alarm mission directory.
