Sharpness is a daily in-app score. Review it under Achievements alongside your history, streaks and badges, then visit Nest for your progress with Lark. It is not a sleep score or a medical assessment. A single number does not tell you whether you are safe to drive or work.

## How to use it

1. Finish your morning and open Achievements.
2. Review the score breakdown shown in the app, and the badges you have earned.
3. Look at several mornings in your history. Use badges and Nest as encouragement, not as a health diagnosis.

## What to check

Your Sharpness Score comes from the missions you finish when the alarm rings. The separate brain warm-up is not offered in the current app. Math Problems, Memory Match and Sequence Recall are alarm missions.
