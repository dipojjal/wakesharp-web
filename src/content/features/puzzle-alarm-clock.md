Choose from the missions below, or put up to four in a sequence. This is the iPhone 2.25 selection; Google Play has version 2.9, so Squats and Push-ups are iPhone-only for now. Camera, movement and voice missions need their corresponding permissions and supported hardware.

## How to use it

1. Choose Memory Match, Sequence Recall or Color Clash.
2. Try the difficulty before saving. A harder puzzle is not always a better alarm.
3. Find the card pairs, repeat the pattern, or tap the ink colour when the alarm rings.

- **Memory Match**: Turn over the cards and find every pair.
- **Sequence Recall**: Repeat a tap pattern that grows each round.
- **Color Clash**: Tap the ink color, not the word.
- **Type It Out**: Type out a line word for word, with autocorrect off.
- **Name Five**: Name five things from a category, out loud.
- **Surprise Me**: A different mission every morning.

## What to check

On iPhone, stop it or snooze it and it rings again within 30 seconds, until your mission is done. Google Play’s version 2.9 rings again a minute later, for up to an hour. Your phone’s own controls still work.
