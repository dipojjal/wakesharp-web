Choose from the missions below, or put several in a sequence. This is the public iPhone 2.14 selection. Camera, movement and voice missions need their corresponding permissions and supported hardware.

## How to use it

1. Choose Memory Match, Sequence Recall or Colour Clash.
2. Try the difficulty before saving. A harder puzzle is not always a better alarm.
3. Find the card pairs, repeat the pattern, or tap the ink colour when the alarm rings.

- **Memory Match**: Turn over the cards and find every pair.
- **Sequence Recall**: Repeat a tap pattern that grows each round.
- **Colour Clash**: Tap the ink colour, not the word.
- **Type It Out**: Type out a line word for word, with autocorrect off.
- **Name Five**: Name five things from a category, out loud.
- **Surprise Me**: A different mission every morning.

## What to check

In iPhone 2.14, stopping or snoozing delays the alarm by one minute. It can repeat for up to one hour unless you finish the mission. Your phone’s own controls still work.
