Take real steps, counted by your phone.

## How to use it

1. Choose Walk It Off and allow the motion permission it needs.
2. Try a short route with your phone in your hand or pocket. Check that steps register.
3. Walk carefully when the alarm rings. First Light instead asks you to hold your phone in daylight.

- **Walk It Off**: Take real steps, counted by your phone.
- **First Light**: Walk to a window and hold your phone in the light.

## What to check

Choose from the missions below, or put several in a sequence. This is the public iPhone 2.14 selection. Camera, movement and voice missions need their corresponding permissions and supported hardware. In iPhone 2.14, stopping or snoozing delays the alarm by one minute. It can repeat for up to one hour unless you finish the mission. Your phone’s own controls still work.
