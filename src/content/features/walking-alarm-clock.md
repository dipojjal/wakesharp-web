Take real steps, counted by your phone.

## How to use it

1. Choose Walk It Off and allow the motion permission it needs, or choose Squats or Push-ups on iPhone and allow the camera.
2. Try a short route with your phone in your hand or pocket. Check that steps register. For Push-ups, lay the phone on the floor and let the front camera count.
3. Walk carefully when the alarm rings. First Light instead asks you to hold your phone in daylight.

- **Walk It Off**: Take real steps, counted by your phone.
- **First Light**: Walk to a window and hold your phone in the light.
- **Squats**: Complete squats with on-device camera tracking.
- **Push-ups**: Do push-ups over your phone on the floor, counted by the camera.

## What to check

Choose from the missions below, or put up to four in a sequence. This is the iPhone 2.25 selection; Google Play has version 2.9, so Squats and Push-ups are iPhone-only for now. Camera, movement and voice missions need their corresponding permissions and supported hardware. On iPhone, stop it or snooze it and it rings again within 30 seconds, until your mission is done. Google Play’s version 2.9 rings again a minute later, for up to an hour. Your phone’s own controls still work.
