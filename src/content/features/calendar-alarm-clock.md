“Ring 90 minutes before my first meeting.” WakeSharp reads your calendar on your device, re-checks it overnight, and moves the alarm when the meeting moves. Read-only, optional, never transmitted.

## How to use it

1. Allow calendar access and create a smart alarm rule.
2. Choose how many minutes before the event to ring, plus the earliest and latest times.
3. Review the next alarm after editing your calendar. Keep a fixed backup for an appointment you cannot miss.

## What to check

A shift rotation is for patterns that aren't weekly: 4-on / 4-off from an anchor date, each phase with its own time, and a preview calendar so you can check it before you sleep on it.
